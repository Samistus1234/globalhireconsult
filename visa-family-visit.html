— FAQ —

Processing time?

We confirm your expected timeline at intake, once we've reviewed your documents — it depends on current Saudi processing.

Pricing per visitor?

Per person, all-in: ₦814,500 for ages 1–49, ₦844,500 for ages 50–69, ₦984,500 for ages 70–89. Older tiers reflect mandatory health insurance. Plus a shared ≈ ₦30,000 family documents fee.

Can it be extended or converted to residence?

Family Visit cannot be directly converted to a residence visa. You'd apply separately for Family Residence (also offered).

Refunds?

The $50 USD you pay to start is credited toward your visa total once you proceed, and fully refunded if you're ineligible at intake. Government and processing fees are non-refundable after submission.
