Program: eLab Complete —

1. Program Description

eLab Complete is a guaranteed end-to-end nursing placement program. eLab will manage all processes required to place the Candidate in a nursing role in , including but not limited to:

  • DataFlow verification (education, license, work experience, background check)
  • NMCN fee processing and institutional verification
  • Prometric exam booking and exam fees
  • eLab Academy enrollment for exam preparation (study materials, tutoring, coordination)
  • Professional licensing registration (Mumaris/SCFHS for Saudi Arabia; QCHP evaluation for Qatar)
  • CV preparation and interview coaching
  • Job matching with employers and interview coordination
  • Visa processing support
  • Exam travel support
2. Timeline

eLab commits to completing the entire process — from enrollment to job placement — within 4 to 9 months from the date of enrollment. All stages will run concurrently where possible to minimize total timeline.

3. Payment Terms
  • The Candidate shall make full payment of the agreed program fee prior to commencement of any services.
  • Payment is due upon signing this agreement.
  • Payment activates the Placement Guarantee (Section 4).
  • The program fee covers all services listed in Section 1, including third-party fees paid on the Candidate's behalf.
4. Placement Guarantee

eLab guarantees that the Candidate will be placed in a nursing role in within 9 months of enrollment.

If eLab fails to place the Candidate within 9 months of enrollment, the Candidate shall receive a full refund of the program fee minus Tangible Costs (Section 5) already spent on the Candidate's behalf.

5. Tangible Costs (Non-Refundable)

The following third-party costs, once incurred on the Candidate's behalf, are non-refundable under any circumstance:

  • DataFlow verification fees
  • NMCN fees
  • Institutional verification fees
  • Prometric exam fees
  • Mumaris/SCFHS or QCHP registration fees
  • Exam travel costs (flights, accommodation) if already booked or completed
6. Exam Failure
  • First attempt failure: No refund. eLab will provide free resit preparation through eLab Academy. The Candidate shall pay the exam rebooking fee and eLab's rebooking coordination fee.
  • Second attempt failure: No refund. The Candidate shall pay the exam rebooking fee and eLab's rebooking coordination fee. The program continues.
7. Voluntary Withdrawal

The Candidate may withdraw from the program at any time by providing written notice.

Upon voluntary withdrawal, the Candidate shall receive a refund of the program fee minus:

  • All Tangible Costs (Section 5) already spent on the Candidate's behalf; AND
  • A 15% administrative penalty of the total program fee.
8. Disqualification

eLab may terminate this agreement immediately and without refund if the Candidate:

  • Provides false, fraudulent, or forged documents
  • Fails the required medical screening
  • Is found to have a criminal record not disclosed during consultation
  • Repeatedly fails to meet the obligations set out in Section 9
9. Candidate Obligations

The Candidate agrees to:

  • Attend all scheduled eLab Academy sessions and complete assigned preparation work
  • Respond to all communications from eLab within 48 hours
  • Attend all scheduled employer interviews arranged by eLab
  • Provide only authentic and genuine documents
  • Complete the required medical screening prior to enrollment
  • Not accept placement from any other recruitment agency, employer, or third party outside of eLab's matching process during the duration of this program (Non-Compete — Section 11)
10. eLab Obligations

eLab agrees to:

  • Process all verifications and registrations in a timely manner
  • Provide comprehensive exam preparation through eLab Academy
  • Actively match the Candidate with suitable employers in the selected destination
  • Coordinate visa processing and travel arrangements
  • Provide regular progress updates to the Candidate
11. Non-Compete

During the duration of this program (from enrollment until placement is completed or the agreement is terminated), the Candidate agrees not to:

  • Accept a job offer arranged by another recruitment agency or third party
  • Independently apply for or accept employment in the selected destination outside of eLab's matching process

Violation of this clause shall be treated as Voluntary Withdrawal (Section 7), and the withdrawal refund terms shall apply.

12. Limitation of Liability

eLab's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the program fee paid by the Candidate. eLab shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party government agencies, regulatory bodies, or circumstances beyond its reasonable control.

13. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

14. Entire Agreement

This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. Any amendments must be made in writing and signed by both parties.
