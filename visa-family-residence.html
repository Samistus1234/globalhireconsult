Permanent dependent residency for spouse and children of an Iqama holder. Processed via MoFA + Jawazat.

— What's included —

  • MoFA + Jawazat application by our licensed Saudi visa team
  • Sponsor eligibility check (income, profession category)
  • Document review
  • WhatsApp + email status updates
  • Visa PDF delivered to your dashboard

— What you'll need —

  • Passport bio page + passport-style photo (white background) — per person
  • Sponsor's Iqama (front + back)
  • Court affidavit — consent letter for children, affidavit of relationship for spouse / parents
  • Invitation letter (yellow-paper Resident visa)

Age-based requirements: Children 1–5 do biometric + stamping only. Ages 6–13 add a medical exam. Ages 14+ add a police character certificate. We tell you exactly what each person needs at intake.

— FAQ —

Processing time?

4–8 weeks. Variable by sponsor's profession category, age tier of each dependent, and Jawazat workload.

Pricing per dependent?

Per person, all-in: ₦369,000 for children 1–5, ₦559,000 for children 6–13, ₦604,000 for adults 14+. Plus a shared ≈ ₦30,000 family documents fee for court affidavit and Foreign Affairs authentication. Approximate USD equivalents shown alongside.

What's actually in the price?

Medical (where age-applicable), police character certificate (age 14+), biometric enrolment, document authentication, Saudi visa stamping, and our processing fee. Post-issuance completion fee is included — no surprises after the visa is out.

Refunds?

$50 USD deposit refunded if you're ineligible at intake. Government and processing fees non-refundable after submission.
