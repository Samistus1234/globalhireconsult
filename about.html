About GlobalHire

Solving Healthcare's Biggest Hiring Challenge

GlobalHire was built by eLab Solutions International to address a simple but massive problem: the world needs healthcare workers, and healthcare workers need the world. The systems connecting them were broken.

Our Story

From eLab to GlobalHire

eLab Solutions International LLC started with a mission: use technology to solve operational problems in healthcare. We built systems for hospital management, patient care coordination, and medical education.

But one problem kept surfacing — staffing. Hospitals could not find qualified professionals fast enough. Qualified professionals could not navigate the maze of international credentials, licensing, and visa processes. The recruitment agencies between them were slow, expensive, and often unreliable.

GlobalHire was born from this gap. We combined our healthcare domain expertise with AI and automation to build a recruitment platform that actually works — for both sides of the equation.
