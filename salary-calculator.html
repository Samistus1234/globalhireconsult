Reading a Gulf offer.

Three things decide whether an offer is good, and the headline salary is only one of them.

Basic pay is the number that grows

End-of-service gratuity, overtime and some leave pay are worked out on basic salary only. Two offers with the same total can leave you very different amounts after two years.

Housing is cash or a room

Some employers pay a housing allowance, others give you shared staff accommodation. A room saves you rent but you lose the choice; ask which one before you compare.

Ask for the scale, not just the total

Government hospitals pay on fixed grades. Ask which grade and step you're being offered, in writing, then check it against the published scale before you sign.

How we got these numbers.
