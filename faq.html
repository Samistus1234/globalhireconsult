Employers For Healthcare Institutions

Platform Technical & Features

We cover nursing (all specialties), allied health professions (physiotherapy, occupational therapy, radiology, pharmacy), physicians, dentists, and healthcare support staff. Our verification engine is configured for the regulatory requirements of each profession.
Our matching engine analyses multiple factors: clinical skills, years of experience, specialty certifications, language proficiency, regulatory eligibility for the destination country, cultural fit indicators, and candidate preferences. Each factor is weighted and scored to produce a ranked shortlist.

Trust Compliance & Security

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are GDPR compliant and maintain role-based access controls. Candidate data is only shared with employers you have been matched with. You can request complete data deletion at any time.
If discrepancies are found, the candidate is notified with specific details about what was flagged. Minor issues (expired documents, name mismatches due to marriage) can be resolved by uploading corrected documents. Serious discrepancies result in profile suspension pending resolution.
Verification results are only shared with employers who have been matched with the candidate. Results are presented as a verification score and summary — detailed document contents are not shared unless the candidate explicitly authorises it.
