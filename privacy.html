Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Effective Date: April 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how eLab Solutions International LLC ("eLab", "we", "our", or "us"), operating the GlobalHire platform at globalhire.elabsolution.org, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data. By using our platform, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Full name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, country of residence.
  • Professional information: Specialty, qualifications, years of experience, license numbers, preferred destination countries.
  • Documents: Passport copies, passport photos, educational certificates, professional licenses, DataFlow reports, Mumaris certificates, Prometric results, CVs, police clearance reports, and travel insurance documents.
  • Communication data: Messages sent through the platform, WhatsApp conversations initiated via our links, and email correspondence.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on the platform.
  • Device data: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Recruitment services: Matching you with job opportunities, facilitating applications to employers and healthcare institutions, and managing the placement process.
  • Credential verification: Verifying the authenticity of your professional documents through partners such as DataFlow Group and regulatory bodies.
  • Account management: Creating and maintaining your portal account, enabling document uploads, and providing application tracking.
  • Communication: Sending application status updates, document review notifications, job alerts (if subscribed), and responding to your inquiries.
  • Platform improvement: Analysing usage patterns to improve features, performance, and user experience.
  • Legal compliance: Meeting obligations under applicable data protection laws and healthcare recruitment regulations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Employers and healthcare institutions: When you apply for a position, we share your profile, qualifications, and uploaded documents with the prospective employer for the purpose of evaluating your candidacy.
  • Verification partners: We share relevant documents with credential verification services (e.g., DataFlow Group, Quadra Bay) to authenticate your qualifications.
  • Regulatory bodies: Where required by law or for licensing purposes (e.g., SCFHS/Mumaris, DHA, DOH, NMC).
  • Service providers: Trusted third parties that help us operate the platform, including cloud hosting (Supabase), email services, and analytics tools. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on secure, encrypted servers provided by Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure).
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Uploaded documents are stored in secure, access-controlled storage buckets with per-user isolation.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorised eLab staff on a need-to-know basis.
  • We conduct regular security reviews to identify and address vulnerabilities.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfil the purposes described in this policy. Specifically:

  • Active accounts: Data is retained while your account is active and for 24 months after your last login.
  • Placed candidates: Records are retained for the duration of the placement plus 36 months, as required for compliance and audit purposes.
  • Job alert subscribers: Email and preference data is retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Documents: Uploaded documents are retained for 12 months after your application is closed or withdrawn, unless required longer by law.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@elabsolution.org.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time, where consent is the legal basis.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes, including marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@elabsolution.org. We will respond within 30 days.

7. International Data Transfers

As an international recruitment platform, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These transfers are necessary to match you with opportunities in your preferred destination countries. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place for all international data transfers.

8. Children's Privacy

GlobalHire is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., WhatsApp, employer websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you via email or a prominent notice on the platform. Your continued use of GlobalHire after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
