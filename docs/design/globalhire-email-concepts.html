My recommendation

B · Navy & Gold as the primary direction. It matches the brand’s own serif wordmark, answers “Fortune 500” most directly, and makes the milestone moments feel like documents worth keeping — which is what an offer letter is. The serif display and navy letterhead also pair well with the gold your offer stage already uses.

If B feels too heavy for everyday updates, the clean move is B for milestones + A for routine stages — one shared shell, two skins. Either way I’ll build it as a single template so all ten stage emails, plus the welcome email, inherit the design automatically.
