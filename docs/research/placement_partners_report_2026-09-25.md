## Key findings

- **No major US nurse recruiter publishes a formal overseas sub-agent programme.** The public channel is almost always an *individual* referral bonus (Avant, Interstaff, Conexus, Shearwater, Greenstaff, Westways). Agency relationships are handled privately: Avant's referral T&Cs mention an 'Avant Global Business Affiliate' channel; Conexus, PassportUSA and Avant all run through POEA/DMW-licensed accredited agencies in the Philippines — the template eLab should ask to replicate for Nigeria. The only *published* agent-tier programme found is Kennedy Access Group (EB-3 unskilled, not healthcare) — useful as a model of what to request (agency tier / independent contractor / referral agent, finder's fee).
- **Africa appetite (verified on-site):** Avant (Ghana/Kenya/Nigeria testimonials + Nigerian nurse blog profiles), Conexus (Kenya/Ghana RN & MLS testimonials), Interstaff (dedicated Kenya landing page), MedPro (Kenya cited), Westways (Africa explicitly named), O'Grady Peyton (Nigerian attendees in webinars), Medliant (Nigeria/Ghana/Kenya in visa-bulletin content). **Constraint:** Alliance-certified firms that follow the WHO Safeguard List (PassportUSA/Health Carousel, Global Nurse Force) do not actively recruit from Nigeria/Kenya/Ghana; they take self-initiated applicants case by case.
- **Ethical-recruitment credentials:** Alliance (TruMerit) certification is for *US* recruiters; AAIHR membership is for US recruiting/staffing firms. Overseas partners are expected to be licensed in-country, charge candidates nothing, and comply with the Alliance Code / AAIHR principles. Pack to bring: Global Hire recruiter licence LAB/EW/001637, CAC RC 1949626, EAPEAN membership, written no-fee policy, data-protection statement, client references.
- **Baseline candidate bar:** RN with active licence; 1–2 yrs *current* acute-care bedside (100+ bed hospital for Interstaff / Global Nurse Partners); NCLEX-RN — required up-front by PassportUSA, Wayne, Global Nurse Force, but sponsored by Avant, Conexus, Interstaff, Medliant, Shearwater, Greenstaff; IELTS/OET/TOEFL/PTE for VisaScreen; VisaScreen before consular interview. EB-3 'Rest of World' (Africa) priority dates are currently the fastest-moving category.
- **Physicians:** US firms place IMGs who are already in US residency (J-1 waiver → H-1B). IMG Recruitment is the only one found marketing the new state 'no-US-residency' licensure pathways (FL, TN, VA, WI, IL, ID, IA, LA, MA) relevant to eLab's African doctors. The $100k H-1B fee (Sept 2025 proclamation) is under litigation (Global Nurse Force v. Trump, N.D. Cal.).
- **Trustaff / Medical Solutions / Nurse Fern:** Trustaff is a domestic travel-nurse brand (no international programme found); Medical Solutions' international arm is WorldWide HealthStaff Solutions (listed); Nurse Fern is a remote-nursing job board — excluded.

# REGION: Canada

# Canada — IEN/IMG recruiters & employers that could take an overseas sourcing partner (eLab / Global Hire Consult)

Researched 25 Sep 2026 from live sites (web_search/web_extract/curl). Anything not read directly from the organisation's own site is marked **unverified**. No forms were submitted, no accounts created.

## Key finding — Canada is a *licensed-recruiter* market, not a sub-agent market

Unlike the Gulf/UK, almost no Canadian health employer publishes an "agency partner programme". Public health authorities (NS Health, AHS, NL Health Services, Bayshore) actively warn candidates *against* third parties. The realistic entry points for a Nigerian sourcing partner are:

1. **Become the offshore affiliate of a Canadian *licensed* recruiter** (Saskatchewan law explicitly contemplates "a partner, agent or affiliate of a licensed recruiter"; SHA's 2022 Philippines tender required exactly that).
2. **Feed candidates directly to employer portals** (free, no partner status, no fee) — Health Match BC, SHA EOI pool, Horizon NB, Shannex, Covenant.
3. **Partner with private Canadian IEN recruiters that publish a "Partner with us" CTA** — BeGlobal/TalentX, Talynto, CAN Pathways (physicians), Global Health Employment (NS), Greenstaff Medical Canada (lists Nigeria as a source country).

### Provincial recruiter-licensing rules (who must hold the licence)
| Province | Law | Who must be licensed | Key conditions (verified) |
|---|---|---|---|

| Manitoba | *Worker Recruitment and Protection Act* | Anyone assisting a foreign worker/employer, fee or not; licence only issued to Law Society members or RCICs (CICC) | $100 fee; $10,000 letter of credit; **no fees may be charged to workers**; employers must register. https://www.gov.mb.ca/labour/standards/doc,wrpa-license_info,factsheet.html |
| Nova Scotia | *Labour Standards Code* (foreign worker provisions) | Recruiters need a Labour Standards recruiter licence; employers need an employer-registration certificate and **must use a licensed recruiter** (employers doing own recruitment exempt) | https://novascotia.ca/lae/employmentrights/fw/foreignworkers_labourstandardscode.asp ; list: https://novascotia.ca/lae/employmentrights/fw/licensedrecruiters.asp |
| British Columbia | *Temporary Foreign Worker Protection Act* 2018 | "Every individual who recruits foreign workers for an employer in B.C. must be licensed" (394 licensees listed) | https://services.labour.gov.bc.ca/licensing/tfw_issuancepublication ; apply: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/hiring/hire-temporary-foreign-workers/licensed-recruiter/start |
| Ontario | *ESA* recruiter/THA licensing (since 1 Jul 2024) | Recruiters and THAs; employers may not knowingly use an unlicensed recruiter | $1,500 application fee from 1 Jan 2026 (2-yr licence); $25,000 security; penalties $15k/$25k/$50k. https://www.ontario.ca/page/licensing-temporary-help-agencies-and-recruiters |

**Implication for eLab:** never charge candidates a placement fee for a Canadian job; sign as sub-agent of a licensed Canadian recruiter (SK/NS/BC/ON/MB) or supply candidates fee-free to employers who self-recruit.

## 1. The rules that decide everything (UK Code of Practice, March 2025 revision)

**Source:** gov.uk Code of Practice (updated 27 Mar 2025) — https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/code-of-practice-for-the-international-recruitment-of-health-and-social-care-personnel/code-of-practice-for-the-international-recruitment-of-health-and-social-care-personnel-in-england ; NHS Employers red/amber list — https://www.nhsemployers.org/articles/code-practice-red-and-amber-list-countries ; FAQs — https://www.nhsemployers.org/articles/code-practice-frequently-asked-questions

| Rule | What the text says | Consequence for eLab/Global Hire (Nigeria) |
|---|---|---|
| **Nigeria is RED-LIST** (with Ghana, Cameroon, Zimbabwe, Pakistan, Bangladesh… 54 countries; Kenya & Nepal amber) | "No active recruitment of health and care staff will be undertaken from countries on the red list" unless a government-to-government agreement exists. There is **no UK–Nigeria G2G agreement**. | Any UK employer/agency that *targets* people **resident in Nigeria** breaches the Code. |
| **Residence, not nationality** | "The rules… are based on the country where an individual is residing when they apply… rather than the nationality." Scenario 9: Haitian doctor resident in Canada can be actively recruited. | **Nigerian nurses/doctors living in Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Oman etc. are NOT red-list candidates.** eLab's Gulf-based client base is fully recruitable by UK agencies. This is the single most valuable legal route. |
| **What counts as "active recruitment"** | Advertising in any medium; asking staff to tell friends/family in red-list countries; referral bonuses; **"referring candidates to specific vacancies in the UK in return for a fee from the employing organisation"**. FAQ: "referring candidates who are currently resident in red list countries to an employing organisation, **even if they have approached the agency directly**" is active recruitment; agencies should state on CV-upload pages that red-list-resident applications cannot be accepted. | A UK agency **cannot** accept Nigeria-resident referrals from Global Hire for UK roles. Scenario 4: the agency must signpost the person to NHS Jobs to apply directly. |
| **Direct application (the legal route)** | "A direct application is when an individual makes an application to a specific advertised role on their own behalf… Direct applications do not use a third party." Employers "may consider direct applications from individuals resident in red-list countries"; the vacancy must be "hosted by, and recruited to, the same sponsoring organisation". Scenario 11 (NHS trust advert, Ghana-resident nurse hired = OK) and Scenario 12 (care-home group's own advert, 3 Nigeria-resident hires = OK). | Nigeria-resident candidates can only be placed by **applying themselves** to an employer's own advert (NHS Jobs / Trac / employer careers site / care-home group site). Not via an agency, not via a "recruitment collaboration" (Scenario 3: a provider recruiting for other providers = breach). |
| **Post-offer agency support IS allowed** | "Where a candidate has already been appointed… following an independent direct application… recruiting organisations can support and facilitate the employee's passage to the UK" (Scenario 10). Burden of proof is on the agency. | eLab can legally sell **post-offer services** (NMC/CBT prep, OET/IELTS, visa filing, relocation) to Nigeria-resident clients who already hold a UK job offer. It cannot be paid by the UK employer *for the introduction*. |
| **Fees to candidates** | Illegal (Employment Agencies Act 1973 s.6) for GB agencies; "Any recruiting organisation that sub-contract to agencies outside the UK must not use non-UK agencies that charge applicants fees." | To be a sub-agent to any Ethical-Recruiters-List agency, Global Hire must **not charge candidates work-finding fees**. eLab's licensing/exam-prep services must be contractually separate from job-finding. |
| **Scope** | Applies to NHS **and** "independent or private sector employing organisations", care homes, local authorities, agencies, collaborations and **sub-contractors**; employers must make Code adherence a contractual obligation of sub-contractors. | The private sector / care homes are **not** an exemption. Only the *direct application* route (and postgraduate training programmes) differ. |
| **Out of scope: postgraduate training** | Annex C: appointment to Foundation/specialty training programmes is outside the Code. The **Medical Training Initiative** (MTI, up to 1,500 places/yr, 2-year, prioritised for LMIC doctors) is showcased as a permitted case study. | Doctors: MTI and specialty training posts are a legal channel for Nigeria-resident doctors; PLAB → direct NHS Jobs applications is the other. |
| **Devolved nations** | Scotland/NI have their own Codes with identical red list; Wales follows England. Ethical Recruiters List is UK-wide. | Same rules across the UK. |

**Ireland is different.** Ireland is a WHO Global Code signatory but has **no statutory equivalent** of the UK Code/Ethical Recruiters List. HSE Career Hub says international nurses are recruited "through one of a small number of employment agencies that manage the full recruitment process on behalf of the HSE" (Adaptive, Cpl Healthcare, Kate Cowhig, Servisource, TTM) *or* by applying directly (https://careerhub.hse.ie/int_recruitment/ , https://careerhub.hse.ie/int_agencies/). TheJournal.ie (5 May 2023) reported the HSE **stopped active recruitment from Zimbabwe, Ghana and Nigeria** when they joined the WHO list, while completing existing offers — a voluntary policy, not law. Private nursing-home groups and private hospitals are not bound by HSE policy; Nurse Ireland (agency) states it "does not actively recruit from WHO safeguarding red list countries" but supports candidates "who apply to us independently". Practical read: Ireland's private sector (nursing homes, private hospitals) is the most open legal market for Nigeria-resident nurses via a referral partner, subject to each agency's own WHO stance. NMBI warns that agencies must **not** upload documents or pay fees on the applicant's behalf (https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU).

### How the Ethical Recruiters List works (and whether Global Hire can get on it)
- List: https://www.nhsemployers.org/articles/ethical-recruiters-list (Flourish table; **279 entries** on 23 Sep 2026: recruitment agencies, "recruitment organisations", collaborations, employing organisations). Being on it is a **prerequisite** for the two NHS frameworks (HealthTrust Europe TWS II Lot 1b; NHS Workforce Alliance RM6333, live to 1 May 2027).
- Application (https://www.nhsemployers.org/articles/application-process-ethical-recruiters-list): (1) online form — commitment to the Employment Agencies Act 1973 & Conduct Regulations, business practices, declaration of all associated businesses/references, permission for the **Employment Agency Standards (EAS) Inspectorate** to share inspection data; (2) 15-question knowledge test, pass 13/15, 3 attempts; (3) EAS check; decision ≈10 working days after EAS reply. Spot-checks use **Companies House**, website and social media. Contact: internationalrecruitment@nhsconfed.org. No fee today (one is reserved).
- **Verdict for a Nigeria-based agency:** the process is built around GB-registered agencies (EAS jurisdiction, Companies House). Two Africa-focused agencies are on the list (**Medtours Africa**, **Inspire Africa Group Ltd**) but both appear to be UK-registered entities **[registration not verified]**. Being on the list would *not* permit recruiting Nigeria-resident candidates anyway. Realistic path: (a) form/partner with a UK-registered entity, or (b) act as an **overseas sourcing partner** to a listed agency for candidates resident in green-list countries (Gulf, etc.) under a written sub-contract that bans candidate fees — this is exactly the model Pertemps Medical ("sourcing partners based in… countries") and TTM ("over 50 affiliated suppliers located across India, Philippines, UAE…") publish.

### What remains legal for a Nigerian sourcing partner — summary
1. **Green-list-resident Nigerians** (Saudi/UAE/Qatar/Oman…): full agency referral to any UK agency/employer. **Best commercial route.**
2. **Nigeria-resident candidates → UK:** candidate-led *direct applications* only (NHS Jobs, Trac, trust careers pages, care-home group sites). eLab may coach/prepare (English, CBT, NMC) and may provide **post-offer** relocation/visa services; it cannot be the introducer or be paid a placement fee.
3. **Doctors:** PLAB → direct NHS Jobs applications; MTI/specialty training (outside Code); Remedium etc. will not take Nigeria-resident referrals.
4. **Ireland:** HSE agencies are voluntarily red-list-cautious; private nursing homes/hospitals and agencies such as Nurse Ireland accept independent applicants; candidate must hold an NMBI Decision Letter and do RCSI aptitude test/adaptation; employer sponsors Critical Skills Employment Permit (Atypical Working Scheme during adaptation).
5. **Never** charge candidates a job-finding fee in any of these routes.
