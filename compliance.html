Verification Logic

How We Verify Credentials

Our multi-layered verification process catches discrepancies that manual reviews miss. Every credential goes through a structured authentication pipeline.

Document Authenticity

Uploaded documents are analysed for formatting consistency, metadata integrity, and known forgery patterns. Suspicious documents are flagged for manual review.

Registry Cross-Check

Credentials are verified against 180+ professional registries and regulatory bodies worldwide. License numbers, registration dates, and standing are confirmed.

Consistency Scoring

AI analyses cross-references between documents — dates, institutions, specialties — to identify inconsistencies that could indicate errors or fraud.

Audit Trail

Every verification action is logged with timestamps, methods used, and results. This complete trail is available to employers for regulatory compliance reviews.
