No. 01

Registered Nurse — Qatar (2-Year Contract)

Urgent · Interviews this week Registered Nurse QAR 5,000–6,000 2-Year Contract

Who we're looking for · Hard requirements

  • Female applicant, under 40 years of age.
  • DataFlow verification completed — Primary Source Verification report ready or in final stage.
  • Prometric exam passed, or already scheduled with a confirmed date.
  • Registered Nurse with a current, active nursing licence in your country of practice.
  • Diploma or Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSc Nursing).
  • Hospital bedside experience — Med-Surg, ICU, ER, OR, Paediatrics, Obstetrics, or other clinical units.
  • Clear interpersonal English; comfortable communicating with patients, families, and a multidisciplinary team.
  • Ready to commit to a two-year posting in Qatar and able to travel within weeks of offer.

What you'll do

  • Deliver direct patient care in line with the unit's protocols and Qatari regulatory standards.
  • Work alongside physicians, allied health, and senior nursing staff in a structured clinical environment.
  • Maintain accurate clinical documentation and follow infection-control, medication, and safety practices.
  • Contribute to handover, audits, and continuing professional development as expected of a permanent staff nurse.

Documents you'll need

  • Up-to-date CV (PDF preferred).
  • Nursing licence and academic transcripts.
  • International passport, valid for the duration of the contract.
  • Certificates of employment / experience letters from previous hospitals.
