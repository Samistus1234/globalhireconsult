Questions.

What is the $50 to start?

It opens your case. It's credited to your visa total, and fully refunded if we find you're not eligible at intake. Government and processing fees can't be refunded once we've submitted.

How long does it take?

It depends on current Saudi processing. We confirm your timeline once we've reviewed your documents, usually within 24 hours of receiving them.

I live in Nigeria. Can I get a Tourist or Umrah visa?

Visa-only applications are for people who already live abroad with residence. From Nigeria we arrange full packages: Umrah from Kano with flights, transport and hotel, and Tourist packages on request.

Why do family prices change with age?

Older visitors need health insurance, and some dependants need a medical exam or police certificate. The price covers those, so there are no surprises later.
