Your Journey

8 Stages from Application to Placement

Every step is tracked, transparent, and designed to move you forward as efficiently as possible.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up and upload your CV. Our system organises your qualifications, experience, and specialties into a structured profile.

2

Document Upload

Upload your licenses, certificates, and identification documents. We guide you on exactly what is needed for your target destinations.

3

Credential Verification

Our verification team authenticates your credentials through trusted partners including Dataflow Group and Quadra Bay. This process typically takes 2–4 weeks.

4

Expert Matching

Our matching engine analyses your verified profile against available roles, considering skills, experience, regulatory fit, and your preferences.

5

Shortlist & Interviews

Employers review your verified profile and invite you to interview. We handle scheduling and provide preparation resources for each employer.

6

Offer & Contract

Once matched, you receive a formal offer. We review contract terms with you and ensure everything aligns with local regulations.

7

Visa & Relocation

We guide you through the visa application process, provide relocation checklists, and connect you with local support in your destination country.

8

Onboarding & Support

Your first 90 days are supported with check-ins, integration assistance, and access to our professional development resources.
